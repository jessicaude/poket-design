# Product decisions

Agreed rules. Follow them when building; change them only when the team agrees, then update this file.

## Widgets
- **Three independent flows:** Checkout, Payout and Wallet don't move money between each other.
- **One shared Poket account** across all widgets: sign-in, identity, tier and payment methods are shared.
- **Display:** widgets open as a pop-up over the merchant's page; the Wallet can also be embedded in the page.
- **Branding:** Poket by default; CentryOS theme (blue); white-label with the merchant's name and colours, keeping "Payments by Poket".

## Checkout
- Every payment **under $500** skips the ID check (Tier 1 limits apply). $500 or more needs identity verification.
  For a naira (NGN) order the limit uses the **USD value of the naira total**: under $500 needs no ID check, at Tier 0 too; $500 or more goes to identity verification.
- New users give their **legal first and last name** at sign-up; it prefills identity verification later.
- Terms: https://onepoket.com/terms-and-conditions · Privacy: https://onepoket.com/privacy-policy

## Refunds and disputes (WGT-12)
- The demo user's Checkout payments (naira and dollar, card and bank transfer) are kept on the shared Poket account. They show in **Checkout's Poket account** (Activity, a payment's detail) and in the **wallet's Activity** (the reference's activity row and transaction detail).
- A payment to a store can be disputed **up to 60 days** after it was made ("Raise a dispute" on its detail: "You can raise a dispute for N more days."; after that, contact the merchant or Poket support). Deposits, top-ups, refunds, sends and conversions can't be disputed.
- The dispute flow keeps the reference's screens: what happens and what to have ready, then the form: a **reason** (not received, not as described, charged twice, didn't authorise, other) and a **short description** (both required; contact date and evidence optional), then **review and submit**. A dispute raised in one widget shows open in both, and the merchant is told (payment.dispute_opened, with the reason).
- An open dispute: the payment reads "Dispute open" and "{amount} held while we review".
- A dispute is resolved by **refund** (refunded as a merchant refund: to the card, or a naira bank transfer to the NGN balance), **release** (closed, the merchant keeps the payment, with the reason: "[reason]", not decided) or **reversal** (back the way it was paid: the card, or the bank account a transfer came from). The merchant is told (payment.dispute_resolved, with the outcome) and the user gets a notification. A resolved dispute can't be reopened: a payment is disputed once.
- Two disputes restrict the account from raising more ("You can't raise a dispute right now").
- **Refunds:** a merchant can refund a payment in full or in part. The payment then reads "Refunded" or "Partly refunded", and each refund is its own "Refund · {merchant}" record with a receipt. Card payments go back to the card ("Back to Visa ••• 4242 in [refund time]"); naira bank-transfer payments go to the NGN balance (*to confirm: not in the PRD*). The merchant is told (payment.refunded). A fully refunded payment can't be disputed.
- Ada's demo account is seeded with three Checkout payments, as the reference does: a recent naira card payment, a naira bank transfer from 10 days ago, and a dollar card payment from 76 days ago (outside the dispute window).
- In the demo, Checkout's account has its Activity tab only; Home, Methods and messaging the merchant aren't ported.

- **Report a problem** on a Paid send to a Nigerian bank. "I sent it to the wrong account" requests a recall, saying up front that bank transfers can't always be recovered. It's then submitted, in progress, and either recovered (back in the NGN balance, with a notification) or not recovered ("The receiving bank didn't return it.", with a "Recall closed" notification).

## History and receipts (WGT-11)
- Every transaction has its own record and receipt with a unique reference (copyable), including naira that comes in by itself (deposits, transfers to the account details, delayed transfers, naira from CentryLink users, recovered recalls). Bank transfers also show the bank's session ID (format not decided: "[session ID]").
- Receipts use the same rows, in the same order, for every kind: Status, Date, From, To, Method, Network (stablecoin deposits, sends and conversions), You send, They get, Rate, Refunded, Reversed, Fee, On your statement (card deposits: the statement descriptor), Reference, Session ID, and last a card deposit's highlighted Total charged (amount plus card fee) (only those that apply).
- One template for every transaction's detail, money in or out: the direction icon (+ money in, ↗ money out, ⇄ a conversion; the same icons on activity rows and balance histories, with how it moved in the row's subtitle), the amount, one title line, the rows above (the status only in the Status row, no chip), then Share receipt.
- Notifications open their transaction's detail. The bell opens Notifications from any screen, and Back returns to that screen.
- History is grouped by day and filters by All, Money in and Money out. Conversions show under All only.
- The NGN balance has statements for This month, Last month, Last 3 months or a custom range of up to 12 months, as PDF or CSV: the account holder, the NGN account details if any, the period, opening and closing balances, money in and out, and every transaction with its running balance. Opening plus money in minus money out is always the closing balance.
- Every transaction's receipt, money in or out and in any currency, can be shared (native share, else copied as text) or downloaded as a PDF or image. It carries the wallet's brand (Poket or white-label) and always "Payments by Poket". A receipt for a transaction that isn't done (pending, held, failed, reversed, under dispute) says so at the top.

## Payout (Get paid)
- No identity step up front. Sanctions screening and a payout account name match run on every payout, in the background.
- **Lifetime payouts ≥ $2,000:** keyed-in ID check (ID number + date of birth; no document, no selfie).
- **Lifetime payouts ≥ $10,000:** liveness check (selfie).
- Checks apply on the payout **after** the one that crosses a threshold, and come **after** the payee enters the amount and taps Get paid.
- Rejected or returned payouts don't count toward the lifetime total.
- Destinations: bank (free, 1 to 3 business days) or debit card (instant, 1.5%). Credit cards can't receive payouts.

## Payment methods
- One list shared across widgets. Each method has "Use to pay" and "Use to receive payouts" toggles and a default.
- A method used by an active subscription can't be switched off or removed.

## Wallet
- **Sign-up and log in**: signed out, the wallet shows the welcome screen (Sign Up, Log In; reference/poket-unified.html). Both open Checkout's sign-up screen (reference/poket-widgets.html `SCREENS['signup']`, the same component): the email or phone (with a switch and a country code, Nigeria first), the legal first and last name and Poket's terms, then Continue; the 6-digit code is entered in place, with Edit and Resend. An email or phone that already has an account is spotted as it's typed: "This email (number) already has a Poket account. We’ll send a code to log you in.", the names and terms hide, and the button reads "Continue to log in". There's no separate log-in screen. After the code, a new account gets the location check and is made once it passes: Tier 0, empty balances, on the wallet home. There's no age question at sign-up: the date of birth is given at identity verification. Logging in checks the location each time. Accounts are the one shared Poket account: one made in the wallet logs in at Checkout, and the other way round, by email or phone. An account made with a phone and no email shows its number where the email would be. Wrong codes count toward the account lock.
- **Identity verification (Tier 1)** is one "Verify your identity" screen, shared by Checkout and the wallet (reference/poket-widgets.html `tKycDetails`, `features/auth/IdentityDetailsForm.tsx`): Country, the legal first and last name (prefilled from sign-up), the date of birth (DD, a Month picker, YYYY) and, for the United States, the Social Security number (masked, with Show, and "I don't have an SSN"); then "Continue to selfie check" (US with an SSN: Plaid's selfie) or "Continue to ID verification" (Veriff's ID and selfie), and "Finish later". The wallet shows "Your details / ID verification" above it; Checkout shows its own steps (Account, Identity, Payment). In the wallet it runs from the step-up at Tier 0 and ends on "Identity verified" at Tier 1; Checkout runs it for orders of $500 or more.
- **Under 18** is checked twice. By the date of birth given: the wallet shows "We can't create this account", Checkout "You must be 18 or older". By the ID, once it's checked (its date of birth counts, not the one typed in): "We can't verify this account" (the reference's tDocAgeBlocked), in both. Either block stays on the account, so it can't pay at Checkout or log back in to the wallet. Demo triggers: the "ID check" control's "Under 18 at date of birth" and "ID shows under 18", or a legal last name containing "minor".
- Deposits and top-ups can't be disputed.
- Card-funded money is tagged **FIAT-ONLY**: it can be spent or withdrawn, but can't become stablecoin.
- **One naira balance:** a user has one NGN balance, an NGN e-wallet. There's no merchant-held naira (no second NGN balance, no MERCHANT-HELD tag). Every NGN e-wallet can send naira to Nigerian banks within its tier limits.
- Instant transfers (WGT-09): a user can switch instant naira transfers to banks on or off and set a daily limit, up to their tier's maximum (not decided: ₦[tier max]). Raising the tier raises the maximum. New users start with them on or off by default (a product setting, shown as a demo control). Turning them on needs the PIN; turning them off only a confirmation, and it's immediate. Changing the daily limit needs the PIN: a lower limit applies at once; a higher one starts after a cooling-off period (not decided: [cooling-off]) and can be cancelled until then. Sends to Nigerian banks count toward the daily limit (all of today's, whatever paid them, except failed or reversed ones); one that would go over it can't be sent, and the amount screen says how much is left today. While off, sends to Nigerian banks don't go (the amount screen says so, with Turn on); CentryLink users, conversions and checkout aren't affected.
- Account lock (WGT-10): too many wrong verification codes (sign-up, log-in and device codes, in Checkout and the wallet, counted together; the number isn't decided) lock the account for 2 hours. While locked, no money moves anywhere in the wallet, and Checkout can't sign in. The merchant is told (account.locked, with the reason).
- Selfie check before sends (WGT-10): a send over the step-up amount (₦[step-up], judged on what leaves the balance), or one flagged as unusual, asks for a selfie check before the PIN. Failed selfie checks count toward the same 3 attempts as other ID checks; after the 3rd, the send is held for review and the account is blocked (no money moves; contact support).
- Device binding (WGT-10): the first time money moves on a new device (a send, or turning on instant transfers), the user confirms the device with a 6-digit code sent to their email. The device is then bound (listed as "This device" under Security) and isn't asked again; known devices never are.
- Single active device (WGT-10), *to confirm (not in the PRD)*: binding a new device would sign out every other device, so only one is ever signed in; a signed-out device is asked to sign in again on its next action. Off by default in the demo.
- Partner downtime (WGT-13): while the naira partner is down, naira bank transfers are paused. A banner says so on the wallet home, the NGN balance and the affected naira screens. Deposits by bank transfer and new NGN account details are paused (convert from USD instead; no accounts are created); existing account details stay, marked Paused ("Transfers may arrive late."); sends to Nigerian banks can't be made (name enquiry unavailable), and ones already Sending stay Sending until it's back. Checkout marks naira bank transfer Paused; cards still work. CentryLink users, Convert and balances are unaffected. When it's back: transfers that arrived while down are credited, each with a notification ("₦X from {sender} added to your NGN balance, after a delay."), and sends that were Sending move on to Paid.
- Where virtual accounts are supported, the NGN balance shows "Get NGN account details" until the user has a dedicated account (BVN or NIN, then terms), then the account's details. At Tier 0 it asks the user to verify their identity first (it doesn't offer a top-up; top-ups stay under Deposit). A rejected account shows "Not approved" with Try again.
- **Deposit** (everything but NGN, which keeps its own flow): choose a currency or a stablecoin on its network. Cash comes by bank transfer to the currency's account details (USD, EUR, GBP, GHS; Tier 1 and the partner bank's terms; USD is reviewed by the bank first, the others are ready at once) or by card (a saved card or a new one in the account holder's name, through Checkout's card form, then bank verification: the bank's own screen asks for the code it sent). Card money is FIAT-ONLY. A stablecoin comes to its deposit address on its network. Deposits count toward the tier's limits (the step-up when over).
- **Card form**: one form for adding a card, shared by Checkout and the wallet's card top-up (`features/payments/CardForm.tsx`): the number with its brand mark and a Luhn check, the expiry as MM / YY in the future, the CVC at the brand's length, a ZIP code for US cards, inline errors as they're found, and the button disabled until everything is valid. The wallet adds the cardholder name, which must match the account holder, and the authorisation to tick. A card top-up then goes through bank verification: the card's bank's own screen in a sheet ("Visa Secure" and the card's last four; "Confirm with your bank").
- **Amount entry** is one component everywhere money is entered (`features/wallet/AmountField.tsx`): Send's You send, Convert's From, the instant transfer daily limit, the card top-up (USD, EUR, GBP, GHS) and "Add naira" (a single-use account's amount). A box with its label, the currency chip with its flag and the amount in the wallet's amount style; what the screen adds (They get, the limit line) goes under it, then its fee or quote card with the button right under that. Tapping the amount opens the keypad, with Done, docked at the bottom of the panel just above the tab bar, with the screen's button directly above it, so nothing needs scrolling; the amounts and the fee card stay at the top (the amount kept in view) and scroll if they need to. Done puts the button back under the fee card.
- **Waiting states** use one pattern (`features/wallet/WaitingScreen.tsx`): the subject's 48px graphic (a currency flag) with a small clock badge; a title and one line; a vertical tracker of the steps (done with a check, the current one with what to expect under it, upcoming in grey; the wallet's brand colour for done and current); then the actions. Account details waiting for the bank's review use it: "Setting up your USD account", "We’ll let you know when it’s ready.", Details submitted, Bank review ("Usually 1 to 2 business days"), Account ready; "Back to wallet", and "Deposit by card" (the card top-up; not for NGN, which has none). The same screen serves any currency whose account is pending (only USD's is reviewed today). Approved while it's open, the tracker completes, then the account details show. The same pattern, with an icon for the graphic, serves the other waits, keeping their copy and demo actions: a recall ("Recall requested": Requested, Bank reviewing, Outcome), a dispute ("Dispute submitted": Submitted, Under review, Decision) and the ID check ("Checking your ID": Submitted, Checking, Result). A recall or dispute also shows its tracker on the transaction's detail, under its status, and completes there once it's answered or decided. Use this pattern for any new waiting state. Still to move: Checkout's "Checking your ID" (`VerifyingScreen` in `features/checkout/screens/IdentityScreens.tsx`, a spinner in Checkout's own styles) should use the shared waiting component later, as the wallet's does.
- **Virtual cards** need Tier 3. A card spends live from one balance (a currency, or a stablecoin on a network), with no top-up; card spend takes the FIAT-ONLY part of a cash balance first. Details (number, CVV, expiry) are behind the eye, and the number can be copied. Freezing asks first and declines new transactions until the card is unfrozen. A card spends within the account's tier limits (shown on the card). The Card tab shows, below Tier 3, what each remaining tier takes.
- Sending to external wallets needs Tier 2.
- Send replaces the wallet's Transfer and Payout actions (not the Payout (Get paid) widget). Sending is recipient-first: pick a saved recipient or add one (bank account, debit card, CentryLink user, stablecoin wallet), then the amount (you send, they get). Withdrawing to your own bank is sending to yourself.
- **First-payment hold:** one rule for every recipient (bank account, debit card, CentryLink user, stablecoin wallet). Only a first payment of $500 or more (the USD value of what leaves the balance, fees included) to a new recipient is held: it's Scheduled and sends after [hold]. Smaller first payments, and every later one, go at once. The user's own accounts (bank accounts and debit cards whose name is verified as theirs, under Your accounts) are never held. Before sending, the amount and review screens say: "To protect you, first payments of $500 or more to a new account send after [hold]." There's no separate quick check.
- Poket (CentryOS) users are "CentryLink users" everywhere: sending to them (the option, "CentryLink · {handle}" on rows, Method "CentryLink" on details, review and receipts) and receiving from them (credits read "CentryLink", the demo bar's "Receive from a CentryLink user", the paused banner's "sending to CentryLink users"). The wallet itself is still Poket.
- Send to a debit card: Visa or Mastercard debit only (credit cards can't receive payouts), paid only from USD, arrives instantly. Fee $6.00.
- US bank sends have one speed: 1 to 3 business days, fee $6.00. No Instant or Wire choice (and no wire address). Naira keeps its one speed.
- Naira to CentryLink users (WGT-07): if the recipient can hold naira, they get naira. If they can't (no BVN or NIN, or not eligible), the naira converts to **USD on receipt**, and the sender sees it before sending ("{First name} can't hold naira. They'll get USD.", what they get and the quote, held 30 seconds). Existing rules still apply: card-funded money can't become stablecoins, and the naira-to-stablecoins block applies (the conversion is only ever to USD).

## Fees
- **Deposit** (a bank transfer to a single-use account or the account details): 0.40%, capped at $4.00 for USD and ₦5,000.00 for NGN. It comes out of what arrives. Stablecoin deposits are free.
- **Card top-up** (USD, EUR, GBP and GHS; naira can't be funded by card): 2.9% + $0.30, charged to the card on top of the amount.
- **Convert:** from USD or a stablecoin, $2.00 (2.00 in the coin) + 0.40%; from NGN, ₦2,000.00 + 0.40%. Charged in the source, out of the amount converted.
- **Send to an external account** (bank, debit card, stablecoin wallet): $6.00 for USD, 6.00 in the coin for stablecoins, ₦25.00 for NGN, on top of the amount. **To a CentryLink (CentryOS) user:** free, end to end: naira that converts to USD on receipt (a recipient who can't hold naira) pays no conversion fee.
- **EUR, GBP and GHS** (*to confirm*): the dollar fees converted at the demo rates: conversions $2.00 + 0.40% (€1.85, £1.57, GH₵31.00 + 0.40%), sends to external accounts $6.00 (€5.56, £4.72, GH₵93.00; UK bank sends included), deposits 0.40% capped at $4.00 (€3.70, £3.15, GH₵62.00; the EUR, GBP and GHS account cards say so), card top-ups 2.9% + $0.30 (€0.28, £0.24, GH₵4.65).
- Fees count in every total, Max and balance. They show on the amount screen, review, receipt and statement (each fee is its own statement line, with its transaction's reference).
- **Checkout** is unchanged: the payer pays no fee; the merchant covers conversion fees.

## Merchants
- A merchant without a logo shows its **initials** on a colour derived from its name, in the same rounded square as a logo.
